I am **Jinyu Tian** (online handle *Tanhhhhtjy*), a third-year undergraduate at the School of Computer Science and Engineering, [Beihang University](https://www.buaa.edu.cn/) (北航).

My research lives at the intersection of **large language models**, **multimodal reasoning**, and **spatial intelligence** &mdash; in particular, how agentic LLMs can plan, reason, and act in physically grounded environments, and how to make these systems both rigorous and useful. My recent work explores agentic evolution for competitive programming ([Solvita](https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15301)).

I am always open to collaboration — feel free to reach out by email or on [GitHub](https://github.com/Tanhhhhtjy).
